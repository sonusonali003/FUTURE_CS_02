# FUTURE_CS_02 – Phishing Detection & Awareness System

## Project Overview

This repository contains the deliverables for **Task 2 – Phishing Detection & Awareness System**, completed as part of the **Future Interns Cyber Security Internship**. The project focuses on identifying phishing emails through safe analysis techniques, recognizing common phishing indicators, assessing potential business risks, and promoting cybersecurity awareness. Publicly available phishing email samples were examined without interacting with malicious links or attachments, ensuring that the assessment was conducted ethically and safely.

---

## Objectives

- Collect and analyze public phishing email samples.
- Examine sender information, email content, links, and attachments.
- Identify common phishing indicators.
- Classify emails based on risk level.
- Assess the business impact of phishing attacks.
- Recommend phishing prevention and awareness practices.
